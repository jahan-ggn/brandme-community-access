Privacy Policy

BrandMe Community Access processes limited merchant and customer information necessary to provide automated access to Discourse communities based on Shopify purchases.

Data We Collect

The app may process the following information received through Shopify APIs and webhooks:

  • Customer email address
  • Shopify store identifier
  • Order and refund identifiers
  • Purchased product identifiers
  • Webhook identifiers
  • Information required to determine the corresponding Discourse community and membership group

The app also maintains operational information such as delivery status, retry attempts, timestamps, and error logs required to operate and troubleshoot the integration.

How We Use Data

Customer email addresses and purchase information are used only to determine and manage access to the appropriate Discourse community.

For qualifying purchases, the app sends the customer's email and relevant product information to the mapped Discourse community so that the customer can be invited or added to the appropriate membership group.

When a qualifying purchase is refunded, the same information may be used to revoke the corresponding community access.

Customer information is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

Customer information is shared only with the Discourse community configured by the merchant for the corresponding Shopify products.

Requests between BrandMe Community Access and Discourse communities are authenticated using HMAC signatures and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

The app does not sell customer personal information or share it with unrelated third parties.

Data Retention

The app retains operational delivery logs and retry records only for as long as necessary to operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

Processed Shopify webhook identifiers used for duplicate-event protection are automatically deleted after 7 days.

Customer information is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required for providing the service or when an applicable Shopify customer or shop redaction request is received.

Data Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect information processed by the app.

Data transmitted between Shopify, BrandMe Community Access, and configured Discourse communities is protected using encrypted connections. Access to application data is limited to systems and personnel required to operate the service.

Privacy Requests

BrandMe Community Access supports Shopify's required privacy and data protection requests.

When Shopify sends an applicable customer data request, customer redaction request, or shop redaction request, the app processes the request in accordance with Shopify's privacy requirements and applicable data protection obligations.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's data processing practices or legal requirements change. The latest version of the policy will be made available through this page.
